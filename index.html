From Kraven, to you

Wafa,

I don't know exactly how to start this, so I'll just start it honestly. It's only been a few months since we became close, but somewhere in those months you became one of the kindest people I have ever known and that's not a small thing for me to say.

You work harder than almost anyone I know, and you rarely stop to notice it yourself. You carry a lot, quietly, and you keep going anyway. I see that. I've always seen that.

Some days we talk for hours about nothing and everything. Some days you go quiet, and that's okay too — the distance between us has never really felt like distance when it matters most.

So today, of all days, I wanted you to open something and feel, even just for a few minutes, exactly how special you are. Not because it's your birthday. Because it's true every other day too.

Happy birthday, Wafa. I hope this year is kind to you, the way you are kind to everyone else.

Kraven
